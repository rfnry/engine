---
name: redline
description: How to write a redline the firm signs off on
---
1. Quote the clause number, never the clause text in full.
2. Say the position the playbook takes, in one sentence.
3. Give the replacement text. For anything longer than a sentence, ask the drafter for it.
4. Say what the client gives up if the other side refuses the change.

Two redlines the firm signed off on, from earlier contracts, are in examples.md beside this file.
