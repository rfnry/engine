---
description: How to rate a departure from the playbook as low, medium or high
---
High: the clause exposes the client to uncapped liability, to a claim it cannot defend, or to losing
the services without notice. Medium: it costs the client money or time it could not plan for. Low:
it differs from the playbook in form, not in what the client bears. Rate each departure, and say the
one fact that decided the rating.
